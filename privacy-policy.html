JUNUB

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Junub ("we", "us", "our", "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and related services in South Sudan and globally.

By using Junub you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who we are

Junub is an AI-first job recruitment platform built for South Sudan, connecting job seekers with recruiters across all 10 states.

  • Operator: Junub Team
  • Contact: support@junub.app
  • Country of operation: South Sudan (services may be accessed globally)

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Account data: name, email address, password (stored hashed), role (job seeker, recruiter, admin), organization name (recruiters only).
  • Resume data: the PDF file you upload, plus the structured fields our AI extracts from it (skills, experience, education, summary).
  • Job posts: if you are a recruiter, the title, description, location, and other job details you publish.
  • Application data: the jobs you apply to, the AI match score, and the application status updates a recruiter sets for you.
  • Recruiter chat messages: messages you send to our AI assistant (Google Gemini) for shortlisting and analysis.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device data: device model, operating system, app version, language, network type. Used purely for diagnostics and crash reporting.
  • Usage data: screens visited, button taps, response times. Used to improve the App.
  • Advertising identifier: when AdMob is enabled, Google may receive a non-personal advertising ID to serve ads. You can reset or limit this ID in your device settings.

2.3 Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your contacts.
  • We do not collect your precise GPS location. We only store the South Sudan state you choose.
  • We do not access your photos, microphone, or files outside of the resume PDF you explicitly select.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account (email + One-Time Password verification).
  • To parse your resume with Google Gemini and match you to relevant jobs.
  • To rank candidates for recruiters using AI match scores.
  • To deliver application-status notifications via in-app inbox, email (SMTP), and optionally SMS (Twilio, where available).
  • To show non-personalized or personalized ads via Google AdMob (where consent is given).
  • To diagnose crashes and improve performance.
  • To comply with applicable laws and to enforce our Terms of Service.

4. Third-party services

We share the minimum necessary data with the following processors:

  • Google Gemini (AI): resume text and recruiter chat messages are sent to Google's Generative AI APIs for parsing and conversation. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: serves ads in the App. Receives an advertising identifier and standard request data. See AdMob & Privacy.
  • Email provider (SMTP — e.g. Gmail, SendGrid, Mailgun): sends OTP and notification emails on our behalf.
  • Twilio (optional, where enabled): sends SMS notifications.
  • MongoDB Atlas / hosted database: stores your account, resume, and application data.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone. Ever.

5. Data retention

  • Account data: retained while your account is active.
  • Resume & applications: retained while your account is active so recruiters can match you to new jobs. You can delete your resume at any time inside the app.
  • Recruiter chat history: retained for 12 months.
  • Logs: retained for up to 90 days for security and debugging.
  • Account deletion: when you delete your account, we erase your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is legally required.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for personalized ads at any time.
  • Export a copy of your resume and applications.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@junub.app. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt — we never see your raw password.
  • All API traffic is encrypted with HTTPS (TLS 1.2+).
  • OTP-based two-step verification is used for all logins.
  • Resume PDFs are stored securely and only accessible to you and recruiters whose jobs you apply to.

8. Children

Junub is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete the account.

9. International transfers

Some of our processors (Google, AdMob, MongoDB) operate servers outside South Sudan. By using Junub you consent to your data being transferred and processed in those regions, with safeguards in place under the relevant providers' standard contractual clauses.

10. Advertising and analytics

Junub uses Google AdMob to display banner ads. AdMob may use the device's advertising identifier to serve relevant ads. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization".
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → toggle off.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest revision. Material changes will be announced inside the App.

12. Contact us

Questions, complaints, or data-rights requests:

  • Email: support@junub.app
  • Subject line: Privacy Request
